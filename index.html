How to use the Groww Reviews Analyser

A one-page weekly pulse from your App Store + Play Store reviews.

1 · Export your reviews

Pull your latest App Store / Play Store review export (public review data only — no scraping behind logins) as a CSV.

Required columns, in any order, with these exact header names: date, platform, rating, title, text. Dates can be YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY, or DD/MM/YYYY — the tool normalizes them. Extra columns are fine and ignored. Do not include usernames, emails, or reviewer IDs.

2 · Upload the CSV

Click the upload box on the left, or use "Reload sample data" to go back to the bundled example. The tool keeps the most recent 8–12 weeks and drops anything older.

3 · Review the themes

Each review is bucketed into one of five themes by keyword match, with a 12-week trend sparkline per theme.

Onboarding — signup, verification, first run
KYC — identity docs, approval delays
Payments — transfers, UPI, failures
Statements — history, exports, PDFs
Withdrawals — payouts, limits, fees

Reviews that don't clearly match a theme are grouped as "Other" and excluded from the weekly note.

4 · Generate the note

Click "Generate weekly note" to pick the top 3 themes, select 3 representative quotes, and write 3 action ideas — capped at 250 words, no PII.

5 · Draft the email

Click "Draft email" to turn the note into a ready-to-send email. Copy it into your mail client — this tool does not send email itself.

Constraints this tool follows

Only public review exports · max 5 themes · note capped at ≤250 words · no usernames, emails, or reviewer IDs in any output.
