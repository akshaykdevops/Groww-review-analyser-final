# Weekly App Review Pulse
**Week of Sep 7–13, 2026** · App Store + Play Store · 12 reviews analyzed

## Top 3 Themes
1. **Statements** (4 reviews, 3 negative) — missing transactions and slow/failed exports are the loudest complaint this week, a jump from its usual 1–2/week baseline.
2. **Onboarding** (3 reviews, 2 negative) — phone/OTP verification is stalling new users and the signup form is seen as too long.
3. **Payments** (3 reviews, 1 negative) — mostly positive on speed, but one report of a transaction stuck in "pending" with no visibility.

## Real User Quotes
- *"I can't download my monthly statement as PDF, the export button just spins forever."* (Statements, 1★)
- *"I got stuck on the phone number verification screen for almost 20 minutes, had to restart the app twice."* (Onboarding, 2★)
- *"A transaction has been stuck in pending status for two days now, no explanation given."* (Payments, 1★)

## Three Action Ideas
1. **Fix statement data gaps** — audit the export pipeline for dropped transactions and add a reconciliation check before generating monthly statements.
2. **De-risk OTP verification** — add a visible progress indicator, an auto-retry/resend option, and a fallback path when phone verification stalls.
3. **Add pending-transaction visibility** — surface a status/ETA banner and auto-trigger a refund or retry when a payment is stuck longer than a few minutes.
